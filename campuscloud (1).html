Replacement policy

A predictable two-level decision.
1. LFU first
Find the smallest access frequency in the cache.
2. LRU for ties
Among files with that frequency, remove the one accessed least recently.
3. Efficient structures
Hash map for direct key lookup; frequency buckets with doubly linked lists for recency and eviction.
